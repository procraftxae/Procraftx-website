Restoration & Furniture Painting

Furniture Restoration & Painting in Dubai, Sharjah & Abu Dhabi

This is specifically for furniture that's structurally fine but worn, dated, or damaged in finish — it's deliberately not the same service as furniture cleaning (which addresses dirt and stains, not the wood or finish itself) and not the same as our custom furniture design & build service (which creates new pieces rather than restoring ones you already own). If a piece is sound but looks tired, this is the right service; if it's genuinely broken or you're after something new, those are different pages.

The process depends on what the piece needs: sanding back to bare wood for a full refinish, or a lighter touch-up if the existing finish is mostly intact but chipped or faded in spots. Color and finish matching is standard — we sample against the piece (or the rest of a matching set) before committing to a full refinish, so a single restored chair doesn't end up visibly different from the rest of the set. Antique and vintage furniture gets a gentler process specifically, since aggressive sanding can remove character (and value) that a newer piece wouldn't have.

Small pieces are usually done within days; larger or more detailed restorations take one to two weeks, and we'll give you a realistic timeline before starting, not an optimistic one. What's not included: furniture with structural damage beyond repair — broken joints, rotted wood, or similar — we'll tell you honestly if a piece is better replaced than restored, rather than taking the job and delivering a weak result.

We quote the restoration once we've seen the piece in person, and stand behind the finished result with our 100% guarantee on materials and workmanship.

What's Included

  • Sanding and full refinishing for worn wood furniture
  • Lighter touch-up for mostly-intact finishes
  • Color and finish matching to existing pieces or sets
  • Gentle process for antique and vintage furniture
  • Honest structural assessment — repair vs. replace
  • Pickup and delivery for off-site restoration work

Furniture Restoration & Painting

What's the difference between this and your custom furniture build service?+

This restores furniture you already own — sanding, staining, painting worn or dated pieces. Custom build creates new pieces from scratch. If your piece is sound but tired-looking, this is the right service.

Can you match the color exactly to the rest of my furniture set?+

Yes — we sample against the piece or the rest of a matching set before committing to a full refinish, so a single restored chair doesn't end up visibly different from the set.

Is this safe for genuinely antique furniture, or will it lose its character?+

Antique and vintage pieces get a deliberately gentler process, since aggressive sanding can remove character and value that a newer piece wouldn't have — we treat these differently from a standard restoration.

How long does a typical restoration take?+

Small pieces are usually done within a few days; larger or more detailed restorations take one to two weeks. We give you a realistic timeline before starting, not an optimistic one.

What if my furniture is broken, not just worn — can you still fix it?+

If the damage is structural — broken joints, rotted wood — we'll assess honestly whether it's worth restoring or genuinely better to replace, rather than taking the job and delivering a weak result.

Do I need to bring the furniture to you, or do you work on-site?+

Most handyman and moving work is on-site, but larger restoration or painting jobs are usually collected and returned once finished — we'll confirm which applies when we quote.

Can you refinish in a completely new color instead of matching the old one?+

Yes — if you'd rather update the look than match the existing finish, we can refinish in a new color or stain of your choice.

Is the finished piece covered by any guarantee?+

Yes — every restored piece is covered by our 100% guarantee on materials and workmanship.
