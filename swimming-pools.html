Swimming Pool Construction & Design

Swimming Pool Design, Construction & Maintenance in Dubai, Sharjah & Abu Dhabi

A new pool build runs through the same stages regardless of size: 3D design and planning around your garden's actual footprint, excavation, shell construction, waterproofing, and a tile or mosaic finish — infinity-edge, overflow, and plunge designs are all variations on this same process, differing mainly in the edge detail and how the filtration system is routed. For a typical villa garden, sizing is less about a standard dimension and more about what fits comfortably alongside the rest of the outdoor space — we'll walk the garden with you and recommend a size and shape rather than starting from a fixed template.

Equipment is where pools succeed or fail long-term: filtration and pump sizing, heating or chilling units if you want the pool usable outside peak summer, underwater lighting, and any water features are all specified upfront rather than retrofitted, since retrofitting almost always costs more and can compromise the shell's waterproofing if done carelessly. Decking, coping stones, and the surrounding landscaping are part of the same project rather than a separate scope, so the whole area gets finished together instead of the pool sitting done while the surrounding ground is unfinished for weeks.

We also renovate ageing pools — leak detection, structural repair, tile and grout replacement, and equipment upgrades — and back both new builds and renovations with scheduled maintenance (cleaning, water testing and chemical balancing, filter servicing). What's not included: pools requiring a permit variance for setback from the boundary wall — we check this during the site assessment before design work starts, not after.

What's Included

  • Infinity-edge, overflow, and plunge pool designs

Swimming Pool Construction & Design

Do you build pools from scratch, or only renovate existing ones?+

Both. We handle full new builds from excavation through finishing, as well as renovation and resurfacing of ageing pools.

What pool styles can you design?+

Infinity-edge, overflow, and plunge pool designs, among others — we'll plan the layout around your space and budget in 3D before construction starts.

Do you fix leaks in an existing pool?+

Yes, leak detection and structural repair are part of our renovation work.

What's included in ongoing pool maintenance?+

Cleaning, water testing and chemical balancing, and filter servicing, plus equipment upgrades when needed.

Can you add features like lighting or a jacuzzi to an existing pool?+

Yes — underwater lighting, jacuzzis, and water features can be added during a renovation, not just in a new build.

How long does building a new pool take from start to finish?+

A standard residential pool typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from excavation to first fill, depending on size and finish.

Do you handle the decking and landscaping around the pool too?+

Yes — surrounding decking, coping stones, and landscaping are part of the same project, so the whole area is finished together.

What size pool can you build for a typical villa garden?+

It depends on your available space — we'll assess your garden and recommend a size and shape that fits comfortably, from compact plunge pools to full-length lap pools.
