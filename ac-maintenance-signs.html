Signs Your AC Needs Servicing Before Summer

Last updated: November 2026

Weak or no airflow

If air barely comes out of the vents, or one room is noticeably weaker than others, it's often a clogged filter, blocked duct, or a failing fan motor — all things that get worse (and more expensive) the longer they're ignored.

Blowing warm or room-temperature air

This usually points to low refrigerant, a failing compressor, or a frozen evaporator coil. It's rarely something to fix yourself safely — refrigerant work specifically needs a licensed technician.

Unusual noises

Grinding, rattling, or squealing from the unit usually means a loose or worn part — fan belt, bearing, or motor mount. Catching this early is almost always cheaper than waiting for a full breakdown.

Bad odors from the vents

A musty smell usually means mold or mildew buildup in the ducts or drain line; a burning smell means stop the unit and call a technician immediately — that's an electrical warning sign, not a routine maintenance item.

Water pooling or leaking around the unit

A clogged condensate drain line is the most common cause, and it's a quick fix if caught early — left alone, it can cause ceiling or wall water damage.

Rising electricity bills with no change in usage

A unit running harder than it should to hit the same temperature is a strong, easy-to-check sign that something inside needs attention before the hottest months push it further.

When to book a service visit

If you noticed any two of the signs above, or it's simply been over a year since the last service, book a visit before summer rather than during it — AC technicians are busiest exactly when units are most likely to fail.
