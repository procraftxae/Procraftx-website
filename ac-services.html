Air Conditioning (AC) Services

Air Conditioning (AC) Repair & Servicing in Dubai, Sharjah & Abu Dhabi

Helpful guide: Signs Your AC Needs Servicing Before Summer

AC problems get priority scheduling over almost everything else we do, because in this climate a unit that's not cooling isn't a minor inconvenience — it's usually an urgent call. The most common issues are weak or warm airflow, a unit that's running constantly without actually cooling the room, water leaking or pooling around the indoor unit, and unusual noise from the outdoor compressor. We diagnose before quoting a fix, since a weak-airflow complaint and a warm-air complaint usually point to different problems — a blocked filter versus low refrigerant, for instance — and treating the wrong one wastes your money.

We service split units (the most common setup in villas and apartments), central systems, and window units, each with different access points and failure patterns — a central system fault is often at the air handler, not the vents themselves, while a split unit issue is usually isolated to either the indoor or outdoor component. Filter cleaning and coil cleaning are routine maintenance; gas (refrigerant) top-ups and leak repair are a different tier of work, since a refrigerant leak means there's a specific point of failure to find and seal, not just a top-up to perform, or the gas leaks out again within weeks.

We recommend a service visit before peak summer rather than during it, since that's when units fail most and technicians are busiest — the honest best time to book is when the unit is still working but overdue (see our guide to AC warning signs for what to watch for). What's not included: full unit replacement is handled as its own separate quote, not a servicing call — we'll tell you clearly if a unit is beyond economical repair rather than patching it repeatedly.

Every visit — servicing or repair — gets a clear price before we start, and the work is covered by our 100% guarantee on parts and labor.

Air Conditioning (AC) Services

My AC is running but not actually cooling the room — what's wrong?+

This usually means low refrigerant, a blocked filter, or a failing compressor — all different problems with different fixes. We diagnose first rather than guessing, since treating the wrong one wastes your money.

How often should I get my AC serviced?+

We recommend a service visit before peak summer at minimum, and more often if the unit runs heavily — see our AC warning-signs guide for signs it's overdue sooner than that.

Why is water leaking from my indoor AC unit?+

This is almost always a clogged condensate drain line, which is usually a quick fix if caught early — left alone, it can cause ceiling or wall water damage over time.

Is it worth repairing an old AC unit, or should I just replace it?+

Depends on the unit's age and what's actually failed — we give you an honest assessment during the visit rather than repeatedly patching a unit that's genuinely beyond economical repair.

Do you service central AC systems, or only split units?+

Both, plus window units — central systems, split units, and window units all have different access points and failure patterns, and we service all three.

Can you refill AC gas, or do you need to find the leak first?+

We find and seal the leak first wherever possible — a top-up without fixing the leak means the gas escapes again within weeks, so a top-up alone isn't a real fix.

Why does my AC make a loud noise when it starts up?+

Usually a loose or worn part in the outdoor compressor unit — a fan blade, bearing, or mounting. Catching it early is almost always cheaper than waiting for it to fail completely.

Do you offer priority or emergency AC callouts?+

Yes — AC issues get priority scheduling over most other work we do, since in this climate a unit that's not cooling is rarely something that can wait.
