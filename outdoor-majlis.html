Outdoor Majlis Services

Majlis Design, Construction & Renovation in Dubai, Sharjah & Abu Dhabi

A majlis project in the UAE usually starts with one decision: glass, traditional, or sandwich panel. Glass majlis suit villas that want a modern, light-filled look and work well as an extension of the main house; traditional Arabic majlis lean into tented or heavily-clad designs with detailed finishes for a more formal guest space; sandwich panel majlis trade some of that visual flexibility for faster build times and better built-in insulation, which matters more than people expect once summer arrives.

Whichever structure you choose, the build itself covers the same ground: foundation and framing, full interior fit-out (seating, flooring, wall cladding, ceilings, curtains), insulation and AC servicing so the space is actually usable through summer rather than just decorative, and lighting and electrical work sized for how you'll actually use it — everyday family majlis wiring looks different from one built for hosting large groups regularly.

We also renovate existing majlis rather than only building new ones — re-cladding, roof repairs, waterproofing, and a full interior refresh are common requests for majlis that are structurally fine but dated or worn. What we don't do: majlis on land without a confirmed boundary or the necessary approvals — we'll flag anything that needs a permit during the site visit rather than after the fact.

Outdoor Majlis Services

What's the difference between a glass majlis and a traditional Arabic majlis?+

A glass majlis is a modern, fully-glazed structure with a sleek aluminum frame, while a traditional Arabic majlis uses classic tented or clad designs with detailed finishes. We build both.

Can you build a majlis on any property?+

In most cases yes — the design just needs to suit your property's space and layout. We'll assess the site and advise on any approvals needed before we start.

How long does building a majlis take?+

It depends on the size and finish — a straightforward pergola can take days, while a fully fitted-out glass or traditional majlis can take a few weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline after the site visit.

Do you only build new majlis, or also renovate existing ones?+

Both. We handle full renovations and expansions too — from re-cladding and roof repairs to a complete interior refresh.

Can you add features like a fireplace or sound system to my majlis?+

Yes — we fit bespoke features including smart lighting, sound systems, fireplaces, coffee stations, TV units, and custom storage, either during construction or as a retrofit.

Do you handle ongoing maintenance after the majlis is built, or just construction?+

Both — beyond construction, we cover ongoing maintenance like roof repairs, waterproofing, repainting, AC servicing, and electrical or plumbing work.

Can a majlis be insulated for year-round use in summer heat?+

Yes — insulation, AC servicing, and weatherproof enclosures are all part of what we can build in, so your majlis stays usable through summer.

What's involved in a sandwich panel majlis compared to a glass one?+

Sandwich panel majlis use insulated pre-fabricated panels for faster build times and better insulation, while glass majlis prioritize an open, modern look — we'll help you weigh the trade-off for your space and budget.
