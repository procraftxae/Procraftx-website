Water Tank Cleaning

Water Tank Cleaning in Dubai, Sharjah & Abu Dhabi

Household water tanks need cleaning roughly every 6-12 months — sediment, algae, and bacteria build up over time even with treated municipal water, and the usual trigger for calling is discolored water, an odd smell or taste from the tap, or simply knowing it's been too long since the last clean. This isn't optional maintenance the way some home upkeep is — stale tank water affects every tap in the house, not just one fixture.

Overhead and underground tanks are cleaned differently — overhead tanks (common on villa rooftops) are drained, scrubbed, and pressure-rinsed with direct access, while underground tanks need the same result through a confined access point, which changes the equipment and time involved. Either way, the process is: drain the tank, pressure-clean the interior walls and base to remove built-up sediment and biofilm, sanitize, then refill — done in a single visit for most residential tank sizes.

We check the tank's condition while we're in there, not just clean it blind — cracks, rust on a metal tank, or a failing lid seal are the kind of issues that cause the next buildup to happen faster, and we'll flag anything we see rather than just cleaning around it. What's not included: tank structural repair itself (a cracked tank or failing seal) — we'll identify it and can quote that as a separate job, but a cleaning visit isn't where that repair happens.

The price is agreed before we start based on tank size and access, and the work is covered by our 100% guarantee.

Water Tank Cleaning

How do I know if my water tank needs cleaning right now?+

Discolored water, an odd smell or taste from the tap, or simply knowing it's been over 6-12 months since the last clean are the usual signs — don't wait for all three before booking.

Do you clean overhead and underground tanks the same way?+

The result is the same — drained, pressure-cleaned, and sanitized — but the process differs: overhead tanks have direct access, while underground tanks need the same result through a confined access point, which changes the equipment and time involved.

Can one visit finish the whole job, or does it take multiple days?+

Most residential tank sizes are done in a single visit — drain, pressure-clean, sanitize, and refill, start to finish.

Will you tell me if my tank itself has a problem, like a crack?+

Yes — we check the tank's condition while cleaning, not just clean it blind. Cracks, rust, or a failing lid seal get flagged honestly, since those issues make buildup happen faster.

Do you repair a cracked tank, or only clean it?+

A cleaning visit identifies the issue but doesn't repair it — we can quote tank structural repair as a separate job if that's what's needed.

Is the water safe to use immediately after cleaning?+

Yes — once the tank is drained, cleaned, sanitized, and refilled, the water is safe for normal household use right away.

Can this be scheduled alongside disinfection or pest control?+

Yes — if you're addressing multiple things at once, mention it when booking and we'll coordinate the visits.

How much does tank size affect the price?+

Tank size and access (overhead vs. underground, how confined the access point is) are the main factors — we confirm the price after seeing the tank, not from a general estimate alone.
